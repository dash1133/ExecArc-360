# ARC360 Build Plan — Agreed Scope by Stage

Authoritative source: `Commercial_Terms_Agentic_Exec_Development_v3_20260902.pdf`,

discussion purposes*.

**The review cycle has nine stages.** The earlier concept video was built around a
seven-step spine; that spine is superseded by the nine below.

| # | Review cycle stage | Stage 1 · target 26 Sep 2026 | Stage 2 · target Jun 2027 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Launch the Cycle** | Administrator launches the cycle and nominates respondents; employee and evaluator notified | Informal feedback requests prompted by collaboration signals — max three touches per person a year, no formal write-up |
| 2 | **Self-Evaluation** | Employee rates themselves against competencies; evidence attached manually | Automated evidence sourcing through email and chat |
| 3 | **360 Survey Prep** | Evaluator reviews nominations and confirms the final respondent list | Agent-generated respondent lists from search plus nominations |
| 4 | **Feedback Collection** | Anonymous AI-led feedback interviews; transcripts stored securely | Prior-cycle feedback referenced during interviews |
| 5 | **Evaluator Preparation** | Two drafts anchored to this cycle only — no historical trend view | Write-ups show development across review cycles |
| 6 | **Evaluatee Preparation** | Full employee-facing review and goal-setting | — |
| 7 | **Feedback Discussion** | **Deferred — no meeting transcript capture** | Live meeting transcript capture |
| 8 | **Development Planning** | Goals sourced from Evaluator Preparation | Goals drafted from discussion transcripts |
| 9 | **Track Progress** | Mobile progress tracking against the plan | — |

## Stage 3 · target Oct 2027

Adaptation of the mid-cycle review experience for the year-end review process,
spanning the whole cycle rather than any single stage:

- Adjust workflows, prompts and outputs to support year-end requirements for the full firm
- Incorporate lessons and feedback from the mid-cycle rollout
- Configure the experience for year-end evaluations and goal-setting

## Notes

- Stage 2's informal-feedback capability attaches to **stage 1, Launch the Cycle** —
  it is not a separate surface outside the cycle.
- Stages 6 and 9 have no Stage 2 additions; they ship complete in Stage 1.
- Stage 7 is the only stage **deferred** in Stage 1 — it exists in the cycle, but
  with no system capability behind it until Stage 2.
